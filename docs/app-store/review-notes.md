# Notes for App Review

Open Tether is a free tethering app for photographers: a camera connected by
USB-C or Wi-Fi sends each photo to the iPhone or iPad as it is taken, where it
can be reviewed, rated, given a look and exported. There is no account, no
purchase and no server.

**Reviewing without a camera:** on the Connect screen, tap **"No camera? Try
a demo session"**. Sample frames arrive one by one as if a camera were
shooting. From there you can:

- tap a frame in the filmstrip, rate it with the stars, flag it as a pick;
- apply a look (Looks button);
- open Client Review (More → Client review; hold "Hold to exit" to leave);
- export to Photos or Files (Export button);
- end the session (End, top right) and find it again under Sessions.

A screen recording of a real session with a camera is attached.

**Permissions:**

- Camera: controls a camera connected by cable (shutter, live view,
  exposure). The iPhone's own cameras are never used.
- Photos: reads photos from the connected camera and saves exports.
- Local network: finds Wi-Fi cameras and receives photos cameras upload by
  FTP to the app.
- Location: only so iOS reveals the Wi-Fi network name (to tell whether the
  phone is on the camera's network). Location is never stored or sent.

Camera brand names in the app identify compatible equipment only; the app is
not affiliated with or endorsed by any camera maker, as stated in Settings →
Privacy & legal.
