# Open Tether support

Open Tether tethers your camera to your iPhone or iPad over USB-C or Wi-Fi,
and receives photos from cameras that upload over FTP. It is free, with no
account or subscription.

## Getting connected

- **USB-C:** switch the camera on with a memory card inserted (iOS ignores
  cameras with no card), turn the camera's Wi-Fi and Bluetooth off, and use a
  data-capable USB-C cable straight into the phone. If your camera doesn't
  appear, try disconnecting and reconnecting the USB-C cable.
- **Wi-Fi:** join the camera's network in iOS Settings, then open Open Tether.
- **FTP:** point the camera's FTP upload at the address shown in the app.

No camera handy? Tap **No camera? Try a demo session** on the Connect screen.
