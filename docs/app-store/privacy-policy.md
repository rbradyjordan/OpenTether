# Open Tether privacy policy

_Last updated: 17 October 2026_

Open Tether is a free camera tethering app made by CalendTree, LLC. It has no
account, no servers, no advertising, no analytics and no in-app purchases.

## What we collect

Nothing. Open Tether does not collect, store on our side, sell or share any
personal data, and it contains no tracking or third-party SDKs.

## What stays on your device

Photos you capture or import, ratings, tags, looks, sessions and settings are
kept in the app's own storage on your iPhone or iPad. They leave the device
only when you export them to a destination you choose: the Photos library, a
folder in the Files app, or an FTP / FTPS server you configure.

## Permissions and why they are asked for

- **Camera (connected camera):** to control a camera you connect by cable
  (shutter, live view, exposure settings).
- **Photos:** to read photos from a camera you connect by cable, and to save
  the photos you export. If you grant full access, exports are also filed
  into an album named after the session.
- **Local network:** to find cameras on the Wi-Fi network you are connected
  to and to receive photos they send to the app.
- **Location (while using the app):** iOS only reveals the Wi-Fi network name
  to apps with location access. Open Tether uses it to tell whether you are
  on your camera's network. Your location is never recorded or sent anywhere.

## Children

Open Tether is a general-audience photography tool and does not knowingly
collect data from anyone, including children.
