Try

  • Capture ring: tap to fire. The ring pulses, a frame arrives in the filmstrip with a download ring, then the canvas crossfades to it.
  • Keep current selection: switch the segment, fire again. The canvas holds; a count badge appears on the filmstrip instead.
  • Rate: tap or drag across the stars. Pick and Reject sit either side; a reject dims the frame to 40 %.
  • Exposure chips: tap one. The wheel is the camera's own list; scrolling snaps and applies when you stop.
  • Pull the cable: tap the status pill. The canvas dims, the pill turns red, and a sheet says what to do in one sentence.
  • Client review: the ▭ button. Black, image only. Hold the bottom-right corner to leave.

Rules this follows

  • True black behind the photograph; one accent, used only for "live".
  • Numerals are monospaced and big enough to read at arm's length.
  • Primary actions in the bottom 40 %. Capture is 72 pt and centred.
  • Springs: snap 0.28 s, arrive 0.42 s, settle 0.55 s. Reduce Motion collapses them.
  • Nothing says "done" before the bytes are on disk.
